# Ape Vice Arcade

## The world

**Ape Vice Arcade** is Gameorilla's after-dark game world: neon city lights,
palms, water, music, clever social competition, status, and the playful vices
of a chill ape with a night to spend. It is grown-up in tone, not explicit,
aggressive, childlike, or real-money gambling.

Gameorilla remains the public brand and domain. Ape Vice Arcade is its branded
universe and product language.

## Bananas

Bananas are Gameorilla's in-world points. They are earned through play and
used for in-arcade expression and progression. They have no cash value, cannot
be withdrawn or transferred for money, are not prizes, and are not directly
sold at launch.

Bananas are distinct from PoundTown Games tokens:

- **PTG tokens** are the paid host entitlement for eligible games.
- **Bananas** are a Gameorilla game/reputation balance.

### Initial earn rules

- Finish a game: participation bananas.
- Win a round: bonus bananas.
- Win a game: winner bananas.
- Host a completed room: host bananas.
- Complete a featured challenge: limited bonus bananas.

Exact award amounts should be tuned after real play data, not advertised as
cash-equivalent value.

### Initial uses

- Profile flair and title cards.
- Cabinet skins and harmless cosmetic effects.
- Social status and collection unlocks.
- Future Ape Vice Arcade events.

Do not use bananas to obscure PTG token pricing, create a cashout expectation,
or make an outcome feel like a wager for real-world value.
